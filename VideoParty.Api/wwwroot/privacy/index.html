VideoParty Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

VideoParty is a personal app for watching videos together. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Data we collect

VideoParty does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, location, or contacts.

When the app crashes or encounters an error, a crash report is automatically sent to Sentry, a third-party error-tracking service. These reports may include:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Stack trace of the error
  • Anonymous device identifier

This information is used solely to diagnose and fix bugs. It is not shared with any other third party. You can review Sentry's privacy practices at sentry.io/privacy.

Data we do not collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • No location data
  • No contacts or media library access
  • No advertising identifiers

Data retention

Crash reports are retained by Sentry according to their standard data retention policy. No other user data is stored.

Children's privacy

VideoParty is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
